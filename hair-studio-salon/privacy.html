Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: JUNE 25, 2026

At Hair Studio Salon, located near The Dot Coaching Centre, Model Town, Rewari, Haryana, we prioritize the privacy and security of our clients. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you visit our website, use our style diagnostic tools, or make an appointment.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you interact with our website. This includes:

  • Appointment Details: Name, phone number, email address, selected services, and any special requests or notes you submit during booking.
  • Style Finder Quiz Data: Answers you provide about your hair type, concerns, and style preferences to offer personalized service suggestions.
  • Contact & Inquiry Info: Information submitted via our website inquiry form or when subscribing to our newsletter.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To schedule, manage, and confirm your salon appointments.
  • To communicate with you regarding booking confirmations, reminders, or changes via telephone, SMS, or WhatsApp.
  • To display customized recommendations generated by our interactive Style Finder quiz.
  • To address inquiries, messages, and feedback submitted through our contact form.
  • To send periodic newsletters, grooming tips, and promotional salon discounts (only if you opt-in to subscribe).

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement robust, industry-standard security measures to safeguard your personal details against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Since all bookings on our website are processed without online payment requirements (payments are settled in-person at the salon), we do not collect, process, or store credit card numbers or other sensitive financial data.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We respect your privacy and do not sell, trade, rent, or distribute your personal information to third-party marketers. We may only share basic contact details with trusted software service providers who assist us in managing SMS/WhatsApp confirmations or web hosting, subject to strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You have full control over your personal data. At any time, you may request to:

  • Access or review the contact details we hold for your salon profile.
  • Correct or update any incorrect information (such as a typo in your phone number).
  • Request that we delete your booking history or client contact information from our databases.
  • Unsubscribe from our marketing emails or newsletters by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link or contacting us directly.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our data practices, or wish to update your details, please contact us at:
